# How AI is Transforming Customer Service at Virgin Media O2
By [Tom Chapman](https://aimagazine.com/author/tom-chapman)
January 19, 2026
![](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/138/b4708f3aa904e5a3cb58eb2caa337474:e76239745d250e92cdc597122869264c/gettyimages-2205045668.webp)
Virgin Media O2 saved customers more than 400,000 hours of call time in 2025. Picture: Getty Images
Through AI-powered upgrades and internal team restructuring, the [telecoms operator](https://aimagazine.com/ai-strategy/mwc25-fujitsu-cto-on-5g-goals-telco-transformation) saved customers more than 400,000 hours of call time over the course of 12 months.
![](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/668/084a6d19d730682cf238d3aa1d001fdd:c8420d4283debe3973e487fcbd1b98a6/alan-stott-credit-aws.png)
The system uses natural language understanding (NLU), a type of AI that recognises speech and analyses intent. It helps identify what the caller needs and automatically routes the call to the most relevant agent without multiple handovers.
By shifting to this method, the company reduces unnecessary steps, aiming for faster first-time resolution.
![Youtube Placeholder](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/Z_WQz6LG_WE/hqdefault.jpg)
## Internal restructuring reduces transfers further
Alongside AI-based changes, Virgin Media O2 has also reviewed its internal team structure using performance data. Heatmaps showing where transfers occur most allow the company to identify pain points and streamline call handling.
The result is a smaller number of handovers between departments, as agents are now better positioned to handle a broader set of queries.
The shift follows the cross-training of around 5,000 support staff. Each trained agent can now deal with a wider range of customer issues, helping reduce transfers and improves consistency across service touchpoints.
Alan continues: “Where a customer does need to speak to us, we want to make their experience as simple, efficient and productive as possible. That is why we’ve introduced [AI technology](https://aimagazine.com/artificial-intelligence-ai) which allows a customer to explain the reason for their call at the outset and quickly routes them to the appropriate team."
![](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/668/5fbfc9f0a819f9c634e07c664a2a1f45:53ff176a8b035d6d236b21c7e694efcf/gettyimages-1494104649.jpg)
Natural Language Understanding (NLU), a type of AI that recognises speech and analyses intent, is supporting Virgin Media O2's change \| Photo: Getty
## Customer experience and complaints improve
The company's overall strategy focuses on cutting friction and improving resolution speed – resulting in a measurable improvement in service quality.
By removing 1.3 million call transfers from its support journey, customers were saved 1.45 billion seconds, 24 million minutes or more than 400,000 hours.
Alan concludes: “We’ll continue to invest in our people and systems throughout 2026 and beyond to ensure we’re consistently giving our customers the best possible service.”

# How NVIDIA Became One of the World's Most Valuable Brands
By [Saffron Humphreys](https://aimagazine.com/author/saffron-humphreys)
January 20, 2026
![](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/138/0150bfd04d34ca578f71f4d6af95e8c4:5eb42791314d05761b3ff0d688dd0eb5/gettyimages-2248009084.webp)
Alison will lead NVIDIA's global marketing and communication teams (Credit: Getty)
Brand Finance's latest rankings reveal NVIDIA is the world's fifth most valuable brand, climbing four places and overtaking TikTok and Facebook
A report released by Brand Finance reveals the 2026 ranking of the world's most valuable brands.
NVIDIA has secured a position in the top five, reflecting the rise of AI and the company's role in powering global AI infrastructure.
The brand has overtaken social media giants TikTok and Facebook in brand value, rising from ninth place in 2025 to fifth place in this year's rankings.
Its value is placed at US$184.3bn, which is US$30.8bn more than Tiktok and US$77.2bn more than Facebook.
A leading AI supplier, it also places sixth in the Brand Finance 2026 ranking of the world's strongest brands.

## NVIDIA's climb to success
The brand value of NVIDIA has more than doubled since 2025, up +110%.
It became the leading player in the business of AI hardware as while other companies were selling individual chips, NVIDIA sold entire systems.
With the Deep GPU Xceleration (DGX) line, NVIDIA created an AI supercomputer in a box.
Starting solely as a company focused on 3D gaming graphics, it [became a world-renowned AI operation.](https://aimagazine.com/articles/behind-nvidias-global-ai-strategy-for-success)
NVIDIA's products benefit both personal computing systems and large businesses.
With the DGX, companies can buy a pre-made system instead of making one, which may take several months.
The company also focuses on DPUs (Data Processing Units) for data centres.
While dominating large-scale operations, NVIDIA continues to sell products such as graphics cards and gaming cards, offering a personalised experience to individual customers.
NVIDIA caters to different types of customers at varying scales, guaranteeing its growth, with its focus on AI software and hardware ensuring its business strategy has space to develop.
