## A background rooted in science and ambition
Demis' influence is grounded in a rare combination of academic depth and entrepreneurial vision.
Trained in neuroscience and computer science, his early career was shaped by a fascination with human cognition and the mechanics of intelligence itself.
This interdisciplinary approach has defined his work, enabling him to draw inspiration from the human brain to develop novel AI systems capable of tackling complex problems.
In 2010, Demis co-founded DeepMind alongside Shane Legg and Mustafa Suleyman, with the bold ambition of building general-purpose learning machines.
That ambition has remained consistent throughout his career. His contributions to science were formally recognised in 2024, when he was [awarded a share of the Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://aimagazine.com/articles/alphafold-2-the-ai-system-that-won-google-a-nobel-prize) for his work on protein structure prediction, underscoring the real-world impact of AI beyond digital applications.

## Building Google DeepMind into a global force
DeepMind first captured global attention with AlphaGo, the AI system that defeated world champions in the game of Go.
The achievement was widely seen as a watershed moment for AI, demonstrating the power of deep learning to master tasks previously thought to require human intuition.
## Driving real-world impact
More recently, Demis has overseen innovations with profound implications for science, healthcare and industry.
The tool has been hailed as one of the most significant scientific breakthroughs enabled by AI.
![](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/668/e54c7925d3b2d86c6dc7f9a9e6f7013d:cdbd8025151791d88e4e72f5df8c61ad/demis-1.jpg)
Demis was awarded a UK knighthood for ‘services to artificial intelligence’ in 2024
Beyond AlphaFold, Google DeepMind has continued to expand its influence. The company collaborated with Google to launch Gemini AI at the end of 2023, described at the time as a new breed of AI system capable of advanced reasoning across multiple domains.
Partnerships, including work with Liverpool Football Club on player analytics, highlight the breadth of applications emerging under Demis’ leadership.
## Leadership, responsibility and the future of AI
Demis' influence is not limited to technological progress. He has consistently advocated for the responsible development of artificial general intelligence, emphasising safety, ethics and global cooperation.
His [UK knighthood for 'services to artificial intelligence'](https://aimagazine.com/machine-learning/demis-hassabis-knighthood-from-prodigy-to-ai-leader) – awarded in 2024 – reflects both national and international recognition of his leadership.
As AI continues to reshape economies and societies, Demis stands out as a leader combining scientific excellence with a clear sense of responsibility.
It is this balance that cements his place among the most influential figures in the AI field.
